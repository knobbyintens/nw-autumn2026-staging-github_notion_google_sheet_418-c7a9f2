# The Long History of the Pencil

Item ID: NW-0056
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Long History of the Pencil', exploring the texture of daily work with an emphasis on plainspoken warmth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Long History of the Pencil follows its characters through a year shaped by the texture of daily work. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
