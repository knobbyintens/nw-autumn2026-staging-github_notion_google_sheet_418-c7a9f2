# On the Ethics of Remembering

Item ID: NW-0011
Section: Essays
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A essays work centred on 'On the Ethics of Remembering', exploring the weight of small decisions with an emphasis on slow-burning tension.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, On the Ethics of Remembering follows its characters through a year shaped by the weight of small decisions. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
