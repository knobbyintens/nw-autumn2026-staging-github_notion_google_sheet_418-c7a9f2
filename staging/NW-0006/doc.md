# Midnight at the Ferris Wheel

Item ID: NW-0006
Section: Fiction
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'Midnight at the Ferris Wheel', exploring quiet resilience with an emphasis on historical depth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Midnight at the Ferris Wheel follows its characters through a year shaped by quiet resilience. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
