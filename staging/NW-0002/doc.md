# A Season of Borrowed Light

Item ID: NW-0002
Section: Fiction
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'A Season of Borrowed Light', exploring inheritance and repair with an emphasis on dry humour.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Season of Borrowed Light follows its characters through a year shaped by inheritance and repair. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
