# Notes on Translating Silence

Item ID: NW-0043
Section: Translations
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A translations work centred on 'Notes on Translating Silence', exploring belonging and departure with an emphasis on lyrical restraint.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Notes on Translating Silence follows its characters through a year shaped by belonging and departure. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
