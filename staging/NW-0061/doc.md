# What the River Kept

Item ID: NW-0061
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'What the River Kept', exploring memory and distance with an emphasis on precise sensory detail.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, What the River Kept follows its characters through a year shaped by memory and distance. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
