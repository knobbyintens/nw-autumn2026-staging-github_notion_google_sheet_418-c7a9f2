# A Kitchen Conversation with Chef Marlowe

Item ID: NW-0029
Section: Interviews
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A interviews work centred on 'A Kitchen Conversation with Chef Marlowe', exploring maps and misdirection with an emphasis on rhythmic prose.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Kitchen Conversation with Chef Marlowe follows its characters through a year shaped by maps and misdirection. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
