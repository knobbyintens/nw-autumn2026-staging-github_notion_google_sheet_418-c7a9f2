# A Practical Guide to Slow Reading

Item ID: NW-0053
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'A Practical Guide to Slow Reading', exploring maps and misdirection with an emphasis on rhythmic prose.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Practical Guide to Slow Reading follows its characters through a year shaped by maps and misdirection. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
