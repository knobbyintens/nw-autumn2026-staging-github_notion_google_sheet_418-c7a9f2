# The Orchard Keeper's Almanac

Item ID: NW-0203
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Daniel Okafor
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Orchard Keeper's Almanac', exploring archive fragments and the quiet records that outlast their makers.

The Orchard Keeper's Almanac moves between ledger entries, marginal notes and oral history, building a portrait of ordinary work that is rarely written down. Its method is patient, and its rewards are cumulative.
