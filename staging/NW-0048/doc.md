# A Bear Called Bramble

Item ID: NW-0048
Section: Children's
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A children's work centred on 'A Bear Called Bramble', exploring place and identity with an emphasis on generous curiosity.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Bear Called Bramble follows its characters through a year shaped by place and identity. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
