# Pip's Pocketful of Stars

Item ID: NW-0050
Section: Children's
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A children's work centred on 'Pip's Pocketful of Stars', exploring inheritance and repair with an emphasis on dry humour.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Pip's Pocketful of Stars follows its characters through a year shaped by inheritance and repair. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
