# Between the Shelves: An Interview with Archivist Ndlovu

Item ID: NW-0030
Section: Interviews
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A interviews work centred on 'Between the Shelves: An Interview with Archivist Ndlovu', exploring quiet resilience with an emphasis on historical depth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Between the Shelves: An Interview with Archivist Ndlovu follows its characters through a year shaped by quiet resilience. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
