# In Conversation with Elinora Vale

Item ID: NW-0027
Section: Interviews
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A interviews work centred on 'In Conversation with Elinora Vale', exploring ordinary courage with an emphasis on tender observation.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, In Conversation with Elinora Vale follows its characters through a year shaped by ordinary courage. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
