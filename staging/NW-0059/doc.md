# A Year in the Margins

Item ID: NW-0059
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'A Year in the Margins', exploring the weight of small decisions with an emphasis on slow-burning tension.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Year in the Margins follows its characters through a year shaped by the weight of small decisions. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
