# The Art of Leaving a Room

Item ID: NW-0016
Section: Essays
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A essays work centred on 'The Art of Leaving a Room', exploring the passage of seasons with an emphasis on clear-eyed honesty.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Art of Leaving a Room follows its characters through a year shaped by the passage of seasons. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
