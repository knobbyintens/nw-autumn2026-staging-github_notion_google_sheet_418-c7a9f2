# A Manual for Night Walking

Item ID: NW-0202
Section: Essays
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A essays work centred on 'A Manual for Night Walking', exploring archive fragments and the quiet records that outlast their makers.

A Manual for Night Walking moves between ledger entries, marginal notes and oral history, building a portrait of ordinary work that is rarely written down. Its method is patient, and its rewards are cumulative.
