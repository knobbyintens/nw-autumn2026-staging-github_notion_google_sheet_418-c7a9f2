# A Pocket Full of River Stones

Item ID: NW-0022
Section: Poetry
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'A Pocket Full of River Stones', exploring friendship across generations with an emphasis on unexpected intimacy.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Pocket Full of River Stones follows its characters through a year shaped by friendship across generations. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
