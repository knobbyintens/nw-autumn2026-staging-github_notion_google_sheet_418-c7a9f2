# The Sound Engineer's Long View

Item ID: NW-0028
Section: Interviews
Editor: Daniel Okafor
Summary: A interviews work centred on 'The Sound Engineer's Long View', exploring the passage of seasons with an emphasis on clear-eyed honesty.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Sound Engineer's Long View follows its characters through a year shaped by the passage of seasons. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
