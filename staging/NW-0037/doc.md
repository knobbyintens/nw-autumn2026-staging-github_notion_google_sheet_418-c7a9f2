# The Pleasures and Perils of Open Water

Item ID: NW-0037
Section: Reviews
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A reviews work centred on 'The Pleasures and Perils of Open Water', exploring memory and distance with an emphasis on precise sensory detail.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Pleasures and Perils of Open Water follows its characters through a year shaped by memory and distance. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
