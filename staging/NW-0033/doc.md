# An Interview with the Museum's Night Guard

Item ID: NW-0033
Section: Interviews
Editor: Maya Chen
Summary: A interviews work centred on 'An Interview with the Museum's Night Guard', exploring language and loss with an emphasis on careful argument.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, An Interview with the Museum's Night Guard follows its characters through a year shaped by language and loss. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
