# Letters to a Younger Self

Item ID: NW-0019
Section: Poetry
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'Letters to a Younger Self', exploring belonging and departure with an emphasis on lyrical restraint.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Letters to a Younger Self follows its characters through a year shaped by belonging and departure. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
