# Between Two Tongues: A Translator's Diary

Item ID: NW-0044
Section: Translations
Editor: Daniel Okafor
Summary: A translations work centred on 'Between Two Tongues: A Translator's Diary', exploring the texture of daily work with an emphasis on plainspoken warmth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Between Two Tongues: A Translator's Diary follows its characters through a year shaped by the texture of daily work. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
