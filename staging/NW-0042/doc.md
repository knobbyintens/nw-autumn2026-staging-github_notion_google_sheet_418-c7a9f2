# The Translator's Dilemma

Item ID: NW-0042
Section: Translations
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A translations work centred on 'The Translator's Dilemma', exploring quiet resilience with an emphasis on historical depth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Translator's Dilemma follows its characters through a year shaped by quiet resilience. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
