# The Language of Maps

Item ID: NW-0060
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Daniel Okafor
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Language of Maps', exploring place and identity with an emphasis on generous curiosity.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Language of Maps follows its characters through a year shaped by place and identity. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
