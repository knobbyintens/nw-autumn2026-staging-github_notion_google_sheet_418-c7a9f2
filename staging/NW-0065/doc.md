# The Shape of a Sentence

Item ID: NW-0065
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Maya Chen
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Shape of a Sentence', exploring maps and misdirection with an emphasis on rhythmic prose.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Shape of a Sentence follows its characters through a year shaped by maps and misdirection. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
