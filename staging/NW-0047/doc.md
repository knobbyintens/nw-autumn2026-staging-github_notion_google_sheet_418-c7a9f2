# The Very Patient Caterpillar

Item ID: NW-0047
Section: Children's
Editor: Elinor Vance
Summary: A children's work centred on 'The Very Patient Caterpillar', exploring the weight of small decisions with an emphasis on slow-burning tension.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Very Patient Caterpillar follows its characters through a year shaped by the weight of small decisions. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
