# A Field Guide to Empty Houses

Item ID: NW-0008
Section: Fiction
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'A Field Guide to Empty Houses', exploring the texture of daily work with an emphasis on plainspoken warmth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Field Guide to Empty Houses follows its characters through a year shaped by the texture of daily work. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
