# The Case for Slow Arrivals

Item ID: NW-0040
Section: Reviews
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A reviews work centred on 'The Case for Slow Arrivals', exploring the passage of seasons with an emphasis on clear-eyed honesty.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Case for Slow Arrivals follows its characters through a year shaped by the passage of seasons. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
