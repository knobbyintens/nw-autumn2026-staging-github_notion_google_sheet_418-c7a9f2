# Letters from the Northern Edge

Item ID: NW-0062
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'Letters from the Northern Edge', exploring inheritance and repair with an emphasis on dry humour.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Letters from the Northern Edge follows its characters through a year shaped by inheritance and repair. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
