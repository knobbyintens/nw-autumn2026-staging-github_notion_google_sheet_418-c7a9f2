# The Museum of Unopened Letters

Item ID: NW-0005
Section: Fiction
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'The Museum of Unopened Letters', exploring maps and misdirection with an emphasis on rhythmic prose.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Museum of Unopened Letters follows its characters through a year shaped by maps and misdirection. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
