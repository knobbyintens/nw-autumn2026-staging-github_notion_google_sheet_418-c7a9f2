# The Sound of Snow Remembering

Item ID: NW-0024
Section: Poetry
Editor: Tomas Herrera
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'The Sound of Snow Remembering', exploring place and identity with an emphasis on generous curiosity.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Sound of Snow Remembering follows its characters through a year shaped by place and identity. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
