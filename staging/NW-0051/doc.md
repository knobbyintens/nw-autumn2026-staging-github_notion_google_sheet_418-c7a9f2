# How Cities Remember

Item ID: NW-0051
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Hana Sato
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'How Cities Remember', exploring ordinary courage with an emphasis on tender observation.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, How Cities Remember follows its characters through a year shaped by ordinary courage. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
