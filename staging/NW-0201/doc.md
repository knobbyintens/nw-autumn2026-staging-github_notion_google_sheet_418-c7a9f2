# The Almanac of Forgotten Bridges

Item ID: NW-0201
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Almanac of Forgotten Bridges', exploring archive fragments and the quiet records that outlast their makers.

The Almanac of Forgotten Bridges moves between ledger entries, marginal notes and oral history, building a portrait of ordinary work that is rarely written down. Its method is patient, and its rewards are cumulative.
