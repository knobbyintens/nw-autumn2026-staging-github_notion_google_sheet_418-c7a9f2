# The Cartographer's Last Map

Item ID: NW-0001
Section: Fiction
Editor: Maya Chen
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'The Cartographer's Last Map', exploring memory and distance with an emphasis on precise sensory detail.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Cartographer's Last Map follows its characters through a year shaped by memory and distance. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
