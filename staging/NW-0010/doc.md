# Paper Boats on the Orchard Canal

Item ID: NW-0010
Section: Fiction
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A fiction work centred on 'Paper Boats on the Orchard Canal', exploring friendship across generations with an emphasis on unexpected intimacy.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Paper Boats on the Orchard Canal follows its characters through a year shaped by friendship across generations. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
