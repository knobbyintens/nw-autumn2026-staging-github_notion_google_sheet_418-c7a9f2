# The Mathematics of Everyday Wonder

Item ID: NW-0054
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Mathematics of Everyday Wonder', exploring quiet resilience with an emphasis on historical depth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Mathematics of Everyday Wonder follows its characters through a year shaped by quiet resilience. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
