# The Weather Inside the House

Item ID: NW-0020
Section: Poetry
Editor: Daniel Okafor
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'The Weather Inside the House', exploring the texture of daily work with an emphasis on plainspoken warmth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Weather Inside the House follows its characters through a year shaped by the texture of daily work. The writing leans on precise sensory detail, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
