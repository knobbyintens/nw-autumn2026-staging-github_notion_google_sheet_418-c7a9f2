# Winter Arithmetic

Item ID: NW-0023
Section: Poetry
Editor: Elinor Vance
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'Winter Arithmetic', exploring the weight of small decisions with an emphasis on slow-burning tension.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Winter Arithmetic follows its characters through a year shaped by the weight of small decisions. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
