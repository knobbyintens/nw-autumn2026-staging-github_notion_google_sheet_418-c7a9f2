# Gardens of the Displaced

Item ID: NW-0055
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Elinor Vance
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'Gardens of the Displaced', exploring belonging and departure with an emphasis on lyrical restraint.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Gardens of the Displaced follows its characters through a year shaped by belonging and departure. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
