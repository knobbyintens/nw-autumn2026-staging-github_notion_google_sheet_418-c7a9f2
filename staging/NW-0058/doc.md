# The Bookbinder's Apprentice

Item ID: NW-0058
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Bookbinder's Apprentice', exploring friendship across generations with an emphasis on unexpected intimacy.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Bookbinder's Apprentice follows its characters through a year shaped by friendship across generations. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
