# The Little Lighthouse Keeper

Item ID: NW-0045
Section: Children's
Editor: Priya Raman
Summary: A children's work centred on 'The Little Lighthouse Keeper', exploring language and loss with an emphasis on careful argument.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Little Lighthouse Keeper follows its characters through a year shaped by language and loss. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
