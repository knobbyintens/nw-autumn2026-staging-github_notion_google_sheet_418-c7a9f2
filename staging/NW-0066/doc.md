# A Catalogue of Quiet Things

Item ID: NW-0066
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'A Catalogue of Quiet Things', exploring quiet resilience with an emphasis on historical depth.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Catalogue of Quiet Things follows its characters through a year shaped by quiet resilience. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
