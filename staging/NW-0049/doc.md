# The Secret Door in the Attic

Item ID: NW-0049
Section: Children's
Editor: Maya Chen
Summary: A children's work centred on 'The Secret Door in the Attic', exploring memory and distance with an emphasis on precise sensory detail.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Secret Door in the Attic follows its characters through a year shaped by memory and distance. The writing leans on clear-eyed honesty, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
