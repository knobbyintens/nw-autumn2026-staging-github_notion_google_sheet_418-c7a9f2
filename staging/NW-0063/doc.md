# The Weight of a Small Stone

Item ID: NW-0063
Section: Nonfiction
Editor: Elinor Vance
Summary: A nonfiction work centred on 'The Weight of a Small Stone', exploring ordinary courage with an emphasis on tender observation.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Weight of a Small Stone follows its characters through a year shaped by ordinary courage. The writing leans on unexpected intimacy, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
