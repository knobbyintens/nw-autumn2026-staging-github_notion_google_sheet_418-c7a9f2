# The Gardener Speaks in Questions

Item ID: NW-0026
Section: Poetry
Editor: Ravi Patel
Summary: A poetry work centred on 'The Gardener Speaks in Questions', exploring inheritance and repair with an emphasis on dry humour.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, The Gardener Speaks in Questions follows its characters through a year shaped by inheritance and repair. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
