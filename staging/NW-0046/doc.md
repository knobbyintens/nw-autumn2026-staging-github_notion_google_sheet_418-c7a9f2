# Mimi and the Moon Balloon

Item ID: NW-0046
Section: Children's
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A children's work centred on 'Mimi and the Moon Balloon', exploring friendship across generations with an emphasis on unexpected intimacy.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, Mimi and the Moon Balloon follows its characters through a year shaped by friendship across generations. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
