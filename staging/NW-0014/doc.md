# A Brief History of the Kitchen Table

Item ID: NW-0014
Section: Essays
Editor: Lucas Meyer
Summary: A essays work centred on 'A Brief History of the Kitchen Table', exploring inheritance and repair with an emphasis on dry humour.

Opening on a small, closely observed scene, A Brief History of the Kitchen Table follows its characters through a year shaped by inheritance and repair. The writing leans on lyrical restraint, letting questions accumulate rather than resolve too quickly. What remains at the end is a quiet invitation to look again at the places and people we thought we knew.
